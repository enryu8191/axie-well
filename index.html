    A LITTLE GUIDE

    Room for giants.

    1. Aim & drop. Move your mouse or use A/D or arrow keys. Click or press Space. On touch, drag to aim and release. The Axie above the jar is your current drop; UP NEXT shows the following one.
    2. A little surprise. Start with random drops from the first three merge tiers. Reaching higher tiers adds more drop varieties. Small pieces stay more common; generated drops avoid streaks longer than three. Swapping can change their order.
    3. Merge through 10 tiers. Matching pairs combine into the next puzzle tier, all the way to the Dawn titan. Two titans burst for 200,000 points.
    4. Use your Keeper. Equal-tier merges recharge the Keeper skill. Press E or use the skill button when it is ready. Plant: tap a glowing egg, or press E again to choose the egg nearest your aim. Aquatic: swap your current and next drops. Bird: aim left or right before using Gust. Cancel a target with Escape or the same button. Each Keeper starts fully charged.
    5. Keep it low. A settled piece above the red line for one second ends the run. Merges within half a second build a score multiplier.

    Keepers are guest builds with game-specific skills based on their class and parts. They are not owned Axies and do not earn native AXP. Best score and sound preference stay in this browser.
