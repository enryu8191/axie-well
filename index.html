A LITTLE GUIDE

Room to grow.

  1. Aim & drop. Move your mouse or use A/D or arrow keys. Click or press Space. On touch, drag to aim and release.
  2. Match a pair. Two of the same stage grow into the next. Two adults burst for 800 points.
  3. Keep it low. A settled Axie above the red line for one second fills the garden and ends the run.
  4. Find your rhythm. Merges within half a second build a score multiplier. Raise an adult to unlock hatchling drops.
